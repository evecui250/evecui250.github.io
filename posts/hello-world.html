Hello, Windy Desert

June 12, 2026

Every blog begins with a first post. It is usually awkward — a throat-clearing before the real words arrive. This one is no different.

I chose the name Windy Desert because deserts are not empty. They are full of old light, slow time, and the kind of silence that makes you notice things. The wind just moves it all around.

That is more or less what I want this place to be.

The desert teaches patience to those who do not hurry through it.
